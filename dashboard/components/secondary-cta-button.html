Lower-emphasis call-to-action button for supporting or alternative actions.

Usage

Use for actions that are available but not the primary path (e.g. Forget this device, Cancel, Skip). Often paired alongside a Primary CTA.

Design intent

Offer users a clear secondary choice without competing for attention with the primary action, preserving a single obvious action hierarchy per screen.

Anti-patterns

  • not-for-main-action Do not use for the main action on a screen; that role belongs to the Primary CTA.
  • no-higher-weight-than-primary Do not place a Secondary CTA with higher visual weight than the Primary CTA in the same view.
