Purpose

Top-level (L1) navigation used on surface-level pages, providing device switching and quick access to download activity and the store.

Usage

Used as the L1 navigation on surface-level pages only: homepage, settings page, and Noise AI page.

Design intent

Give users a consistent home-level anchor for switching active devices and reaching high-frequency destinations (downloads, store) without competing with page content.

Anti-patterns
