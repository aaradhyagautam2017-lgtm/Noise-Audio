Purpose

A horizontal progress bar with a content line beneath it, indicating the progress of an ongoing process.

Usage

Used to show that a process is underway and how far along it is, with a short line of content describing it (e.g. "Transferring Firmware").

Design intent

Give ongoing processes a consistent, self-contained progress indicator with a description, so progress states read uniformly wherever they appear.
