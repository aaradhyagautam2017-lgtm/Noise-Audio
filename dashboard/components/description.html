Purpose

A block of descriptive or disclaimer text.

Usage

Used in one of two placements — beneath a card as that card's description, or at the bottom above a CTA as a disclaimer (e.g. "if you turn on this feature, you must turn off the others"). Used to describe a card or feature, or to state a disclaimer.

Design intent

Provide a consistent way to attach explanatory or disclaimer text to a card, feature, or action, separate from headings, so supplementary information reads uniformly wherever it appears.
