Purpose

A section heading that labels a group of cards or a categorized set of items.

Usage

Used to categorize features or parts — a heading placed above a bunch of cards or a categorized group (e.g. labeling a set of Master cards). The label text is always present; the trailing count can be shown or hidden.

Design intent

Give grouped or categorized cards a consistent section label so users can scan and distinguish one category of items from another.

Anti-patterns

  • not-a-card-title Do not use inside a card as its title; that is the heading-content component's role. Card heading labels a group of cards; heading-content sits within a single card.
  • only-for-categorization Do not use for content that isn't a category or section grouping.

note
A count indicator (e.g. "1/2"); its exact meaning is set by the context that uses it.

Rules

  • label-always-present The label text is always present.
  • numeric-is-count The trailing numeric indicates a count.
  • use-cases-owned-elsewhere Use cases are not documented here beyond "categorize features or parts"; the specific categories are defined by whatever uses this heading.
  • not-inside-a-card Card heading labels a group of cards; it is never used as the title inside a single card (that is the heading-content component).
